How do I set up a model?

Settings → API. For cloud, enter Key, Base URL, and model, then Test Connection. For local, install MLX in-app or start llama-server. The composer toggles cloud / local.

How does Work Brief work?

Open You. Fill Who I am and Consider at work. Closing the window saves. Planning, Butler Claw, and Auto Mode all read it.

Auto Mode will not start?

Auto Mode needs a loaded MLX model on Apple silicon. It never uses Groq or llama.cpp.

How do I use Butler Claw?

Switch to Butler Claw, attach files, describe the task and deadline, then confirm the plan onto the calendar. Turn on Tool Loop; choose Until Done to keep going until a deliverable. Writes stay in the sandbox and your chosen folder.

Sync iCloud?

Optional. Settings → Sync iCloud, then Sign in with Apple and upload. Keys, model weights, and workspace files are not uploaded.

How do I erase local data?

Settings → General → Erase Local Usage Data. This Mac only. It does not delete iCloud or sign you out.
