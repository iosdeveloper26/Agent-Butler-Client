Frequently asked questions

How do I set up an AI provider?

Open the app, go to Settings, then API Provider. Choose a provider, enter your API Key, Base URL, and Model, then tap Test Connection.

How does Sync iCloud work?

Open Settings, then Sync iCloud, and sign in with your Apple account. Calendar, chat history, and Butler Claw data can sync through your private iCloud database. Sync is optional. The app works locally without it.

Are my API keys uploaded to the cloud?

No. API keys are stored in the macOS Keychain on this device. They are not uploaded to iCloud or to our servers.

How do I use Butler Claw?

Switch to Butler Claw mode, attach files if needed, describe the task and deadline, review the plan, and confirm it onto your calendar.

How do I erase local data?

Open Settings, then General, then Erase Local Usage Data. This clears local usage on this Mac only. It does not delete iCloud data or sign you out.

What are the system requirements?

macOS on Apple Silicon or Intel. You need an API key from a supported provider, or a local GGUF model for on-device inference.
