What Agent Butler can do

A personal agent butler on your Mac—plan your day, get work done, and talk through anything in natural language.

  • Plan your schedule, boost workflow efficiency

    Describe goals in chat, refine plans with AI, and turn confirmed tasks into calendar events—with reminders and overdue tracking built in.

  • Handle document tasks for you

    Butler Claw runs scheduled work—news digests, summaries, research notes, and other deliverables—then saves results back to your workspace.

  • Free-form Q&A and AI chat

    Ask anything, attach files, and keep context across sessions—powered by your Groq API key or on-device models through llama.cpp.

Data Privacy

Your calendar, chat, and Butler Claw files stay on this Mac unless you turn on Sync iCloud. API keys are stored in the macOS Keychain and are never uploaded.

If you use a cloud model, prompts go to the provider you choose. Local models stay on-device. Read the full Data Privacy page for details.
