Three steps to use

A native Mac app. One purchase unlocks every feature in the app.

01

Install from the Mac App Store

Apple silicon or Intel.

02

Connect a model

Paste your Claude or OpenAI-compatible key, or locally load your download model.

03

Tell the agent what you want

Fill the Work Brief. Talk with your Butler. Your Butler can: 1. Answer your question. 2. Help you finish PC side work (Coding; Writing; CAD...). 3. Help you manage your app (incoming).

What it can do

1

Personalized, executable plans

Use your personal constraints to turn a goal into a plan you can actually follow, register it on the calendar, and break the work into concrete next steps.

2

Manage and excuate PC works

Put PC-side jobs on the calendar.

Excuating PC-side jobs: writing a document, generating code, CAD, and similar file work.

3

Manage your Project

Configure your github.

Merge your codebase to your github.

4

In-app&Agent-assistent file edition

This feature is improving.

This app offers user in-app IDE, which means users can edit and pre-view coding/CAD/pdf files or project, and initially support agent-assistent file edition.

This app also offers In-app Email-box, which means you can use your agent to control your workflow remotely or manage your in-box emails.

5

Multi-Agent Co-operation

This feature is developing.

Describe a workflow you want or select a pre-set agent workflow. You can let a team of agent work for you. Design a plan, excuation, review, manange your project base.

6

Help you operate your app

This feature will be developing.

When you set you app with frontier foundation model likes GPT-6, Claude-5.5 or Grok and muse. Your agent can operate download app in-sandbox.
