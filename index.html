What you can do with Agent Butler

Start with a conversation, walk down the loop step by step, and branch into the tools that fit how you work.

Your intent

One loop from idea to calendar to finished work

  1. Schedule Planning

    Describe what you need in plain language. Review a suggested time slot, then add it to your calendar.

  2. Q&A and Planning

    Chat with attachments, refine a goal, and turn rough notes into batches of tasks.

  3. Butler Claw

    Hand off document work as a scheduled task. The app runs it in the background and saves the result.

  4. Custom workflows

    Shape how the assistant thinks with Agent Loop, skills, and the steps you choose to automate.

Data Privacy

Your calendar, chat, and Butler Claw files stay on this Mac unless you turn on Sync iCloud. API keys are stored in the macOS Keychain and are never uploaded.

If you use a cloud model, prompts go to the provider you choose. Local models stay on-device. Read the full Data Privacy page for details.
