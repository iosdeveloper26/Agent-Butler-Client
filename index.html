What it can do

Agent Butler fits the goals of different people. Everyday users can plan the day and ask questions. Professionals and students can use the simple UI to manage plugins and skills, and extend what Agent Butler can do.

Agent Butler also includes its own MCP and harness, so there is more than one way to reach a goal.
