AI safety

Use the assistant as a capable draft, not as the last word.

Plans can miss the mark

AI has improved quickly, but it can still be wrong, incomplete, or too generic. It only knows what you put in the Work Brief and the conversation, so a plan may not fully match your situation.

You review what matters

Treat schedules, documents, and code as a reference. For anything that affects your time, money, travel, health, or files you will send, check it yourself before you act.
