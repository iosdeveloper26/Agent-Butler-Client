Data Privacy

Built to keep your data on this Mac whenever possible.

What stays on your Mac

Calendar, chats, Work Brief, window memories, Butler Claw files, and preferences stay on this device. API keys are stored in the macOS Keychain and are not uploaded to iCloud or to our servers. Files in your chosen workspace are not included in sync.

iCloud sync

Optional. After sign-in, selected calendar, chat, and profile data can live in your private iCloud database. We do not run a separate backend for chat or calendar. Sync excludes API keys, model weights, and workspace files.

Third-party AI providers

Cloud models send prompts and attachments to the provider you choose (Groq or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint). MLX / llama.cpp inference stays on this Mac.

What we collect

We do not run our own account system, and we do not sell personal data. Support emails are used only to reply and to improve the app.

Your choices

  • You can use the app without Sync iCloud.
  • Erase local usage data in Settings → General.
  • Sign out or change Apple accounts in System Settings.
