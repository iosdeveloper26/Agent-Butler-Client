Data Privacy

Agent Butler Client is built to keep your data on your Mac whenever possible.

What stays on your Mac

Calendar items, chat history, Butler Claw files, and local preferences are stored on this device. API keys are saved in the macOS Keychain and are not uploaded to iCloud or to our servers.

iCloud sync

Sync is optional. If you sign in, selected usage data can be stored in your private Apple iCloud database so you can restore it on this Mac with the same Apple account. We do not operate a separate cloud backend for your chat or calendar content.

Profile fields sent to iCloud are limited to your name and email. API keys are not included.

Third-party AI providers

When you use a cloud model, your prompts and attachments are sent to the provider you choose, such as OpenAI, Groq, Moonshot, Together, OpenRouter, or DashScope. Those requests follow that provider's privacy policy. Local models stay on your Mac.

What we collect

We do not run an account system of our own, and we do not sell personal data. Support emails you send to us are used only to reply and to improve the app.

Your choices

  • You can use the app without Sync iCloud.
  • You can erase local usage data in Settings, then General, then Erase Local Usage Data.
  • You can sign out or change your Apple account in macOS System Settings.
